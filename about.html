Our Story

Built on Vision, Driven by Purpose

Founded in the heart of Port Harcourt, Clover Green Power was born from a bold vision by CEO Peter Essien to create sustainable, reliable, and future-driven energy solutions for Nigeria and beyond.

Driven by innovation and purpose, the company combines clean energy technology with practical infrastructure development to help power homes, businesses, and communities through renewable energy. With CTO Joshua Mkpanam leading the technological direction of the company, Clover Green Power is focused on building smart, scalable, and environmentally responsible energy systems designed for Africa's growing future.

At Clover Green Power, we believe energy is more than electricity — it is opportunity, growth, and transformation. Our mission is to accelerate Nigeria's transition to clean energy by delivering solutions that are sustainable, accessible, and impactful for generations to come.

Our Vision

To become one of Africa's leading renewable energy companies, powering sustainable development through innovation, clean technology, and community-driven solutions.

Our Mission

To develop and deploy renewable energy solutions that improve lives, support economic growth, and contribute to a greener and more energy-secure Nigeria.

Founders

Meet the Team Behind Clover Green Power

Peter Essien

Founder & Chief Executive Officer

Peter Essien is the Founder and Chief Executive Officer of Clover Green Power. Originating from Port Harcourt, Peter founded the company with a vision to drive sustainable energy development across Nigeria and Africa. Passionate about innovation and community impact, he is focused on building renewable energy solutions that promote economic growth, environmental responsibility, and long-term energy stability.

Joshua Mkpanam

Chief Technology Officer

Joshua Mkpanam serves as the Chief Technology Officer of Clover Green Power, where he leads the company's technology and innovation strategy. With a strong interest in modern energy systems and digital infrastructure, Joshua is dedicated to developing smart, scalable, and efficient renewable energy solutions tailored to the needs of Nigeria's evolving energy sector. His focus is on combining technology with sustainability to create impactful solutions for the future.

Roadmap

Our Path Forward

A phased approach to scaling renewable capacity and community impact across Nigeria.

2025

Foundation & Pilot

Establish Port Harcourt headquarters, launch first solar pilot projects, and secure initial partners and investors.

2026

Regional Expansion

Deploy projects across the South-South and South-West; launch biodegradable biomass pilots and community microgrids in underserved areas.

2027

Technology Integration

Scale smart grid and storage solutions; expand CTO-led innovation across new project sites.

2028

National Scale

Grow portfolio capacity, roll out smart grid systems in 50+ communities, and create thousands of green jobs.

2030

Continental Vision

Expand the Clover Green Power model across West Africa with community-driven, sustainable energy at scale.

Why Nigeria Needs Renewable Investment Now

Nigeria loses billions annually to unreliable power. With rising demand and a national push toward cleaner energy, private investment is essential to close the gap. Clover Green Power offers a transparent path for partners to support Nigeria's energy transition — delivering measurable impact alongside long-term growth.
